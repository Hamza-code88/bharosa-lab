# Product Context

## Who is this application for?
The application serves two distinct user groups:
1. **Lab Partners (Local Diagnostic Centers):** Lab owners and managers who need to digitize their operations, receive online bookings for home sampling, and upload test results seamlessly.
2. **Super Administrators:** The Bharosa Lab platform owners/operators who manage the overarching ecosystem, bringing labs and riders onboard, configuring global settings, and managing platform revenue/billing.

## What problem does it solve?
Traditionally, pathology tests require physical visits to laboratories, which poses mobility challenges for patients and leads to wait times. Labs struggle to scale their home-collection services efficiently due to logistical overhead.

**Bharosa Lab Portal solves this by:**
- Providing a digital storefront for labs to offer their test catalog to a wider patient base.
- Equipping labs with a dashboard to track inbound bookings and dispatch localized riders automatically (via the broader ecosystem).
- Automating real-time notifications to labs when new orders are placed.
- Offering Super Admins the tools needed to manage an entire fleet of localized riders and lab networks, resolving operational bottlenecks like manual billing and rider tracking.
